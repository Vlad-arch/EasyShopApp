The app allows you to purchase groceries available at nearby stores. 
There are two types of users.

Shop users can add products they wish to sell by uploading an image and related information, or they can select from existing products. 
For each product, they can manage information such as description, available quantity and price. 
They can also edit profile information such as the address, which will be used to calculate proximity to customers. 
Client users can view and purchase products from nearby stores. They have product search filters and can also select favorites. 
They can also add/edit a product review or view other users' reviews. They can also edit their profile information.
